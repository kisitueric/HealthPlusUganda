About HealthPlus Uganda

Ownership & Leadership

HealthPlus is an independent healthcare institution owned and operated by the HealthPlus Medical Group under the supervision of leading clinical specialists and board directors committed to health innovation.

Our History

Founded in 2012, HealthPlus began as a neighborhood outpatient clinic. Over the past decade, through relentless dedication to patient outcome, it expanded into a multi-specialty medical facility serving over 50,000 patients annually.

Who Can Benefit?

Our doors are open to everyone—families seeking primary care, individuals needing specialized surgical procedures, corporate clients requiring wellness screenings, and emergency medical cases.

What Makes Us Unique?

  • Zero-wait-time emergency triage protocol.
  • Fully integrated digital health records system.
  • Multi-lingual healthcare staff and dedicated patient liaisons.
