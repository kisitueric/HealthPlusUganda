About HealthPlus Uganda

We started to bring good healthcare closer to the community.

Mission: To provide safe, affordable healthcare to all.

Vision: A healthy community where no one fears the health centre.

Who We Serve

Children, mothers, fathers, elders, students and workers.

What makes us unique: We explain illness in simple language.

Our Services

1. General Consultation

Fever, headache, cough, stomach pain. Doctor will examine you.

2. Laboratory Testing

Malaria, Typhoid, Diabetes, HIV. Results in under 1 hour.

3. Pharmacy

Stocked daily with genuine medicine like Paracetamol and Amoxicillin.

4. Maternal & Child Health

Antenatal care, immunization and growth monitoring for babies.

5. Vaccination

Vaccines for children and adults.

6. Emergency & First Aid

Quick care for accidents and sudden illness.
