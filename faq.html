Frequently Asked Questions

Service Related Questions

Do I need an appointment for general consultation?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but scheduling an appointment online reduces waiting times.

Are emergency medical services available 24/7?

Yes, our emergency unit and ambulance team operate round the clock every day of the year.

Medical Center Questions

What are the visiting hours for inpatient wards?

General visiting hours are between 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM daily.

Billing & Insurance Questions

Which insurance providers do you accept?

We accept major health insurance providers including Jubilee, AAR, UAP, Cigna, and Prudential.

Can I request an itemized billing receipt?

Yes, itemized bills are generated automatically upon checkout or accessible via our billing office.

Q: Do I need an appointment?
A: No, you can walk in.

Q: Opening hours?
A: Mon-Fri 8am-5pm, Sat 9am-1pm.

Q: Do you accept insurance?
A: Yes, bring your card.

Q: How much is consultation?
A: Affordable. We tell you price before test.

Q: Is my information private?
A: Yes, only medical team sees it.
